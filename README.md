## Page List
Our project consists of 8 fully integrated pages:
1. `index.html` - Home page with primary navigation and calls to action.
2. `about.html` - Information about the two club locations and working hours.
3. `gallery.html` - Visual showcase of the gym and spa facilities (prepared with a JS modal container).
4. `services.html` - Available passes and programs with direct booking links.
5. `trainers.html` - Profiles of our 6 professional trainers with direct booking links.
6. `contact.html` - Feedback and inquiry form prepared with subject and visit history logic.
7. `booking.html` - Unified booking system for services and specific locations.
8. `colophon.html` - Documentation of technologies and design choices used.

## Three User Journeys
1. **Find locations and schedule:** A visitor lands on the Home page, clicks "Explore the two locations", which takes them to the `about.html` page. There, they read the working hours, learn about the Imanbayeva and Asfendiyarov locations, and click the FAQ link to navigate to the `contact.html` page to ask a specific question.
2. **Explore services and book a session:** A visitor opens the `services.html` page to review the programs. They choose a specific program and click the "Book a Program" button. This leads them to `booking.html`, where they fill out their name, select the club location, and choose a date. Upon clicking "Request Appointment", a prepared confirmation container will display a success message.
3. **Provide feedback about a visit:** A visitor goes to the `contact.html` page to leave feedback about their recent workout. They fill out their contact details and select "Feedback" from the Subject dropdown. This action is designed to trigger the hidden "How many times have you visited us?" question. After selecting "2 to 5 times", they submit the form and receive a visual confirmation in the prepared alert box.

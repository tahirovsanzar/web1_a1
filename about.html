Plan your first visit

  1. Choose a branch and one of the nine programs on the Services page.
  2. Check the opening hours below. Confirm the exact class time and trainer with the club; a proposed time in the planner is not a reservation.
  3. Bring comfortable sportswear, clean indoor shoes and water. Ask whether any other items or identification are needed.
  4. Arrive with a question about your goal or experience level so the trainer can explain which session fits you.

Call your chosen branch to ask which programs and trainers are available at your preferred time, what the price includes, and which items to bring. You can also prepare a question on the Contact page.

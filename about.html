About Experience Fitness

Two Astana locations

Experience Fitness is listed at two Astana addresses. One club is on the fourth floor at Sholpan Imanbayeva Street 8/2. A second listed location is at Sanjar Asfendiyarov Street 6. Both locations are described online as fitness clubs with gym training, and visitors should contact the appropriate branch before travelling because class schedules and reception hours can change.

The Imanbayeva club is listed with group activities such as yoga, stretching, functional training and upper- and lower-body classes. It also lists a gym, cardio area, personal training, massage, sauna and parking. A new visitor should begin by explaining their goal: general health, strength, mobility or a planned event. This helps the club team suggest an appropriate class or trainer.

Training guidance & Our Team

Ask for the right support

Experience Fitness lists personal training and group classes among its services. A personal trainer can help a member learn safe machine setup, plan exercises and monitor technique. A group class gives a scheduled format and can be motivating for people who prefer to train with others. The best choice depends on your experience, schedule and health needs.

Strength training
Exercises that gradually improve muscular strength and control.
Cardio training
Movement that challenges the heart and lungs, such as cycling or treadmill work.
Mobility
Controlled movement that supports comfortable range of motion.

Services and activities

Choose an activity for your goal

Experience Fitness locations are listed with a gym, cardio training and group sessions. The published activity lists include yoga, stretching, functional training, healthy-back classes, power aerobics, upper body and lower body sessions. These options can serve different goals: a gym workout can develop strength, cardio can support endurance and a group class can add routine and instruction.

New members should learn the correct setup of each machine before using it. If an exercise causes pain rather than normal effort, stop and ask a trainer for help. A useful training week can combine strength exercises, gentle mobility work and recovery. Consistency is more important than a single difficult workout. Some listed facilities also include personal training, massage, sauna, parking and a fitness bar; availability can vary by branch.

Suggested first-week plan

  1. Ask reception about the current timetable.
  2. Take a short tour of the gym and changing room.
  3. Try a beginner-friendly session or book an introduction.

What to bring

  • Training shoes
    • Clean indoor shoes are best for the gym floor.
    • Use shoes with stable soles.
  • Water bottle
  • Towel

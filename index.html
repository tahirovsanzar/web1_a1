Experience Fitness in Astana

Move with purpose

Experience Fitness is a fitness-club network in Astana. This student website introduces two listed locations: Sholpan Imanbayeva Street 8/2 and Sanjar Asfendiyarov Street 6. The club information describes gym training, cardio, group classes and personal training. Visitors can use this site to learn where the clubs are, compare membership options directly with the club, read about available activities and send a booking request.

A fitness routine can begin with a small decision: choose a realistic time, arrive prepared and ask for advice when an exercise is unfamiliar. Good technique matters more than heavy weight. A regular plan can be adjusted as a member gains confidence. This website is an informational class project; membership prices, schedules and availability must always be confirmed with Experience Fitness before a visit.

Explore the two locations or request a training appointment.

Before your first visit

Bring comfortable sportswear, clean training shoes and water. Ask the reception team about the current timetable and club rules.

Plan your first session

This request form helps a visitor organize the details needed for a training appointment. It is useful to state whether you want a gym introduction, a personal training session or a group class. Tell the club about any health limitations before beginning an activity. A qualified professional can then advise whether the selected class and intensity are appropriate.

Choose a date and branch that work for you, but remember that a submitted request is not a confirmed reservation. Reception must check trainer availability and the current club timetable. If you are new to exercise, begin at a comfortable level. A short, safe session is a better foundation than trying to complete a difficult program on day one.
